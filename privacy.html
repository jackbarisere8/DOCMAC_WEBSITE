Information used

Docmac collects only the information needed to operate a private account and deliver reliable communications:

  • your phone number for account authentication and sign-in;
  • the username, display name, and optional contact email you choose for your profile;
  • messages, media, and moments you intentionally share within your circles;
  • the connections, circles, and calls you create or join; and
  • technical diagnostic information required to protect accounts, prevent abuse, and resolve crashes.

How it is used

We use this information strictly to provide, protect, and improve Docmac. We do not sell personal information, build advertising profiles, or monetize your private conversations. Your communications belong to you and the people you share them with.

This website and downloads

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not run its own application server, advertising network, tracking pixel, or behavioral analytics service.

  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc., serves this site and may process standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Downloads: APK files are hosted on GitHub Releases. GitHub serves the file and may record aggregate download counts. This website does not receive or store your IP address or device identifiers.
  • Theme preference: Your light, dark, or system choice is stored only in your browser’s local storage. It is not transmitted to us.
  • Feedback: The feedback form opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is submitted to or stored by this website. Your email provider’s privacy terms apply to the message you send.

Retention: This website does not maintain server-side access logs or feedback records. GitHub retains request logs according to its own policies. Emails you send are retained only as long as needed to respond and resolve your request.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. The services used to operate Docmac are:

  • GitHub, Inc.: GitHub Pages hosting and GitHub Releases APK storage and downloads.
  • Google Firebase: Mobile authentication and push notification transport for the Docmac app.

Profile information is visible only to people you connect with in Docmac. If a dedicated release API or feedback service is deployed in the future, this policy will be updated before it begins processing personal data.

We do not use advertising networks, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral cookies. Theme preferences are stored exclusively on your device in local browser storage.

Your rights (NDPA 2023 & GDPR)

In accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable international privacy frameworks (including GDPR/UK GDPR), you retain full rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Portability: You may request a copy of the personal information associated with your account or support emails we hold.
  • Rectification: You may correct inaccurate or incomplete profile information.
  • Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You may request deletion of your account data and support emails by contacting us using the details below.
