Docmac is designed around private relationships. This page explains the information used to provide the app.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This privacy information describes the current Docmac app experience. It should be reviewed with qualified legal counsel before a broader public launch or when app data practices change.

Information used

Docmac uses the information needed to create and operate a private account and its communication features. Depending on how you use the app, this can include:

  • your phone number for verification and account sign-in;
  • the username, display name, and optional contact email you choose for your profile;
  • messages and other content you intentionally send through Docmac;
  • the people, circles, calls, or schedules you create or join; and
  • technical information needed to deliver the app, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures.

How it is used

We use this information to authenticate you, create and display your account, deliver the conversations and features you ask for, protect accounts and the service, and respond to support requests. Docmac is not built around selling personal information or using private conversations to create an advertising feed.

Sharing and storage

Your profile and communications are shared within the Docmac features and relationships you choose to use. Account and app data are processed using Firebase services to provide authentication and cloud-backed app functionality. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the security of Docmac, its users, or the public.

Your choices

You control the profile information you provide and can sign out in the app. To request help with account data or to report a privacy concern, use the support channel below. Do not send passwords or one-time verification codes in a support request.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open a private support report through the Docmac Support page.
